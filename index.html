      Taste your coffee

      Smell it first. Then take a loud slurp: it sprays coffee across your tongue and carries the aroma up to your nose, which is where most of the flavour is.

      Which flavours do you notice? Pick up to 3

      Start broad. "Fruity" is a perfectly good answer. Only get specific when a note is obvious.

      Sour or bitter? Swallow, tilt your head down and open your mouth slightly. If your mouth waters, that's acidity.

      How does it feel in your mouth?

      Sip again in a few minutes. Coffee changes as it cools, and most cups taste clearer once they're just warm.
